About Us

A household partnership built on trust.

Agensi Pekerjaan Jaya Persona SDN. BHD. is a Malaysian maid placement agency headquartered in Klang. We specialise in matching families with trained, vetted domestic helpers — and we stay with you through every renewal, document, and transition.

Every placement goes through rigorous screening: medical checks, skills assessment, character references, and regulatory paperwork. We believe a good match changes lives on both sides of the doorway.

  • ✓ Thorough background screening
  • ✓ Pre-deployment skills training
  • ✓ End-to-end permit handling
  • ✓ Post-placement support
